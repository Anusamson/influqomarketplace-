The Influqo Concept

Direct Sourcing from China for Nigerian Living

Influqo specializes in importing everyday goods directly from vetted manufacturers in China to customers across Nigeria. By streamlining the supply chain and consolidating shipping, we make functional, well-designed lifestyle products accessible without prohibitive retail markups.

From rechargeable electronics suited to Nigerian power realities to durable travel accessories and kitchen organizers, our catalogue focuses on practical value and reliable build quality.

Learn About Custom Sourcing
  1. 1

    Curated Factory Sourcing

    We identify quality manufacturers producing durable consumer goods tested for everyday use.

  2. 2

    Direct Freight Consolidation

    Air and sea freight consolidation lowers overhead and simplifies the logistics journey to Nigeria.

  3. 3

    Doorstep Nationwide Delivery

    Orders are delivered through local dispatch partners to Lagos, Abuja, and all 36 Nigerian states.
