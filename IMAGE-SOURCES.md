# Influqo Image and Brand Assets Documentation

This document records the provenance, format, licensing, and local asset catalog for all visual assets in the **Influqo** client demonstration website.

## 1. Brand Identity & Approved Logos

- **Official Influqo Website Logo (`assets/images/branding/influqo-logo.png` / `influqo-logo-trimmed.png`)**
  - **Source:** Provided directly by client (`media_1794157629361_927feac1.png` / `media_1794154929309_d26a205b.png`).
  - **Artwork:** Approved Influqo brandmark featuring the deep navy rounded square emblem with warm gold arrow, bold navy "Influqo" lettering, and the circular "LTD" mark.
  - **Optimization:** High-resolution transparent PNG trimmed of excessive empty outer whitespace to ensure sharp, prominent rendering (height: 38px–46px) across mobile, tablet, and desktop navigation headers.
  - **Usage:** Canonical website logo displayed prominently across all 11 HTML page headers, mobile navigation drawers, and light-mode surfaces.

- **Light Lettering Logo (`assets/images/branding/influqo-logo-light.png` / `influqo-logo-light-trimmed.png`)**
  - **Source:** Provided in project branding pack (`media_1794154929300_a0c713c6.png`).
  - **Colors:** Cream (`#FAF3E7`) wordmark, Warm Gold (`#F6CD82`) emblem arrow, transparent background.
  - **Usage:** Rendered across dark-mode headers (`[data-theme="dark"]`) and the dark navy footer.

- **Brand Favicon (`assets/images/branding/favicon.svg`)**
  - **Source:** Vector icon derived directly from the approved Influqo square-arrow emblem.

- **Hero Showcase Image (`assets/images/branding/hero-showcase.jpg`)**
  - **Source:** High-resolution commercial studio product photography.
  - **Composition:** Premium arrangement featuring matte black wireless noise-cancelling headphones, an upright smartphone, a sleek digital smartwatch, and a compact dual USB-C fast charger on a dark reflective circular pedestal against a luxury navy gradient with subtle rim lighting and a glowing circular halo accent.
  - **Usage:** Displayed on the homepage split-hero section in `index.html`.

## 3. Storage and Hosting Advantages

1. **100% Local Self-Contained Assets:** Stored directly inside `assets/images/products/`. The prototype works offline and seamlessly in GitHub Pages subdirectory deployments without hotlinking or CDN rate limits.
2. **Standard 1:1 Aspect Ratio:** Displayed inside clean cards styled with `object-fit: cover` and soft rounded corners matching the high-end boutique design.
3. **Easy Production Handoff:** Product managers can easily drop in new supplier photographs into `assets/images/products/` using the matching file name.
